Key Degradation Drivers — Physics Interpretation
🌡️ Temperature
Highest SHAP value (0.38). Thermal stress accelerates SEI growth at T>35°C. Arrhenius dependence: degradation × e^(Ea/R×ΔT).
🔢 Cycle Number
Second driver (0.25). Cumulative capacity loss follows Q(n) = Q₀ - α×n above the knee point. Primary aging metric.
⚡ Internal Resistance Re
Third factor (0.19). Linear growth of Re with cycle # correlates directly with SEI thickness and Li+ transport loss.

ℹ️ NASA ARC-FY08Q4 Dataset

Source: NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence — Ames Research Center
Batteries: B0005, B0006, B0007, B0018 — 18650 LiCoO₂ cells, 2.0 Ah nominal (measured 1.5 Ah)
Protocol: CC/CV charge (1.5A to 4.2V) — CC discharge (2A to 2.7V) — EIS at 10 mHz–20 kHz
Temperature: 24°C ambient (±1°C)
EOL criterion: 30% capacity fade (SOH < 70%) or capacity below 1.4 Ah
Variables: Voltage, Current, Temperature, Capacity (Ah), Time, Re, Rct, Warburg, SOC
Format: MATLAB .mat files — struct array with nested cycle data
Citation: Saha, B. and Goebel, K. (2007). "Battery Data Set", NASA Ames Prognostics Data Repository, NASA Ames, Moffett Field, CA
